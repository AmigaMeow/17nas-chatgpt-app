Reporting a problem

Include the tool you used, the approximate time, your browser and the visible error category. Do not include IP addresses, ASNs, precise location, cookies, credentials or full network captures. A cropped screenshot of the card is enough to start.

Understanding results

An unverified IPv6 probe can reflect a timeout, network condition or browser restriction; it does not prove that IPv6 is unavailable. Country attribution may reflect a VPN or proxy. HTTP duration includes HTTPS and service processing and is not a ping measurement.

The NAT card links to an external test. Return its result to the conversation for interpretation; the card does not itself determine NAT type or the cause of game disconnections.
