Terms of Service — IPv6 Checker & Stats

Draft for operator review. Operator: jiahui zhang. Contact: hello@17nas.com.

This free App checks browser IPv6 connectivity, estimates country, provides a link to a separate NAT test, and displays aggregated IPv6 statistics. It displays browser-observed protocol connectivity and HTTP request duration. Network transport requires transient source-address processing; the App does not retain or display IP addresses, ASNs or city-level location. It does not detect NAT in the card or count unique people or daily queries. HTTP duration is not ping latency; a failed probe does not establish an IPv4-only connection.

Statistics are for reference only. 17NAS samples use a rolling 30-day window and are hidden below 300 samples. APNIC Labs rankings are separate measurements with source date and snapshot country/territory scope. A failed browser probe is unknown, not proof that IPv6 is unsupported.

Network conditions, proxies, geographic classification, caching and provider availability affect results. We do not guarantee uninterrupted service, perfect accuracy or a fixed response time. Do not abuse the service or bypass request limits. We may restrict abuse and revise the service with appropriate notice. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

For questions or corrections contact hello@17nas.com. The interface supports English and Simplified/Traditional Chinese. Japanese, Korean, German, French and Spanish currently use English fallback while professional translations are pending.
