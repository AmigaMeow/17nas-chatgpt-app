# Terms of Service — IPv6 Checker & Stats

Draft for operator review. Effective on production launch. Operator: jiahui zhang. Contact: hello@17nas.com.

This free App checks browser IPv6 connectivity and displays country-level query statistics and IPv6 aggregate rankings. It does not provide IP, ASN or NAT lookup. A country query number counts accepted queries, not unique people. Daily totals reset at UTC midnight; repeated counting is limited within one hour.

Statistics are for reference only. 17NAS's own samples use a rolling 30-day window and are withheld when a country has fewer than 300 samples. APNIC Labs rankings are shown separately with their source date and the countries and territories included in the snapshot. They are different measurements and should not be treated as interchangeable. A failed browser probe is an unverified result, not proof that IPv6 is unsupported.

Network conditions, geographic classification, caching and provider availability can affect results. We do not guarantee uninterrupted service, perfect accuracy or a response time for every request. Please do not abuse the service, bypass request limits or attempt to submit identifiers. We may restrict abusive traffic and revise the service or these terms with appropriate notice. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

For questions, corrections or feedback, contact hello@17nas.com. English and Chinese interface wording awaits operator review; other offered language choices currently fall back to English while professional translation is pending.
